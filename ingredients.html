Ingredients

One Trace Mineral, Five Botanicals

Read this section next to the label rather than instead of it. Per-ingredient amounts are not printed.

Cr

Chromium Picolinate

A trace mineral studied for decades for its role in normal carbohydrate metabolism. The picolinate salt is the form most supplemental research uses.

Related research →

Gy

Gymnema Leaf

Gymnema sylvestre, the Ayurvedic staple nicknamed the sugar destroyer; modern interest centers on gymnemic acids and sweet-taste receptors.

Related research →

Am

African Mango Seed

Irvingia gabonensis seed extract, which reached the research literature through satiety and metabolic-marker studies.

Related research →

Gt

Green Tea Leaf

Camellia sinensis leaf extract, contributing catechins — among the most examined food polyphenols. Naturally carries some caffeine.

Related research →

Mc

Maca Root

Lepidium meyenii root extract, a Peruvian cruciferous food crop with a long traditional reputation for stamina and daily energy.

Related research →

Pg

Panax Ginseng Aerial

The classic adaptogen in its above-ground form — a sourcing choice that gives a gentler extract profile than root material.

Related research →

The Disclosure Most Pages Bury

The Sugar Reset label names all six actives but does not print per-ingredient milligram amounts. That is a real limitation and it is the single most common complaint in customer reviews. It means you cannot compare this formula gram-for-gram against a study — a made-up number would be worse than an honest blank, so you will not find one here.
