Why The Label Doesn't Print Milligrams

This is the single most common complaint in Sugar Reset reviews, so it belongs on the page rather than buried in fine print.

What's Actually On The Label

The Sugar Reset label names all six actives — chromium picolinate, gymnema leaf, African mango seed, green tea leaf, maca root and Panax ginseng aerial — but does not print the amount of each.

What This Means For Comparison

Without per-ingredient amounts, you cannot compare this formula gram-for-gram against a published study. Anyone quoting exact milligrams for Sugar Reset is guessing, not reporting.

Why We Won't Invent A Number

A made-up figure would be worse than an honest blank. What you can verify instead is the identity of the six actives, the manufacturing standard, and the refund window.
